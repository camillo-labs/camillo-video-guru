# Making it yours (step 0)

Two people with the same brief, the same toolkit and the same AI will make the same video unless they choose differently on purpose. Make these choices **before** writing any code, and write them down as a one-page style brief that every later step follows.

## The style brief

| Choice | Questions | Examples of different answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Reference reel** | Which reel do *you* admire, and why? Its structure becomes your structure. | A fast app ad; a slow nature documentary; a news explainer; a talking-head creator; a music video |
| **Story angle and hook** | Who or what is the viewer meant to care about in the first 3 seconds? | A number; a place; **one person** (a climber, a ranger, a local family); an object; a before/after; a question |
| **Visual language** | One world for the whole film. | Footage-led documentary; data and map overlays on footage; illustrated or engraved; bold typographic cards; collage and cut-outs; handheld UGC; split screen; 3D terrain |
| **Type** | Two faces at most: one for captions, one for detail. | Condensed grotesk + serif italic; mono + sans; one heavy display face only |
| **Palette** | Two neutrals, one accent, one warning colour. | Ash grey + ember; forest greens + gold; black and white + one red |
| **Voice** | Whose voice, and at what pace? | Your own (recorded or cloned); a library voice; no voice, text only; a real interviewee |
| **Music** | Which feeling, which instruments, and where does it drop out? | Urgent strings and drums; sparse piano; ambient field recordings; percussion only; silence with sound effects |
| **Signature move** | The one memorable transition or device that belongs to *this* film. | A match cut; one recurring object; a counter; a split screen that closes; a map; a hand drawing over footage |

## Rules

- **Differ on purpose from anyone working on the same brief.** If you know of a classmate's or a reference's specific choices (their comparison, structure, signature move), pick different ones. The facts can be the same; the angle shouldn't be.
- **One language, executed fully, beats several half-done ones.** Mixing styles reads as cheap.
- **Test the brief early.** Render 3–4 stills or a 6–10 s animatic and get a reaction before building the rest.
- **The case study (`case-study.md`) is evidence of what works, not a template.** Take its principles (sound, motion, checks) and leave its look.
