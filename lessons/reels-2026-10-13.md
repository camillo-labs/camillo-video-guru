## A. The sound-effect reels

Twelve named effects, in order: 8 Bit Talking, Old Flash, Cinematic Woosh, Night Vision, Nextel, Digital Text, Riser, Counter, Bicycle Chain, Camera Click, Shine, Woosh. A thirteenth sound closes the reel with no name (the end-card sting, see below). All thirteen are in `sfx/` with an index and a README.

How the names were matched to sounds. Names sit on the waveform strip, mostly above the line, and Digital Text, Bicycle Chain and Shine sit below it, so a crop of only the upper row misses three of them. The waveform scrolls past a centre playhead. Pixel position is not a reliable clock, the strip's scale differs from the label scroll, so the order of names was matched to the order of audio segments instead. The reel has digital silence between most effects, which gives 11 segments at a -50 dB gate. Nextel, Digital Text and Riser run into one segment, and a finer gate at -42 dB with a 30 ms gap splits them at 8.45 to 8.86 s (Nextel), 8.93 to 9.50 s (Digital Text) and 9.50 to 13.25 s (Riser). The last segment, 19.97 to 21.23 s, has no label. It is the same waveform as the boom after the end card of reel 537fad (correlation 0.9997), so it is the end-card sting the reel wrapper adds. It is kept as `outro-hit` with an `end-card-sting` tag.

Loudness. Seven of the 13 clips reach -16 LUFS integrated. Six short sparse clips (old flash, digital text, counter, bicycle chain, camera click, shine) would clip first, so their gain stops at a -1 dBFS peak and they sit between -16.5 and -21.9 LUFS. The index marks them. A uniform -16 LUFS on a click would be a brick-walled click.

### Sync, tested

- Cuts and the voice. All six cuts that fall inside the speech land within 0.07 s of a word start in the transcript. Cut at 3.13 against "do" 3.06 and 3.14, 4.30 against "dream" 4.24, 4.90 against "doesn't" 4.90, 5.63 against "doesn't" 5.66, 6.07 against "enter" 6.06, 6.47 against "my" 6.40. Six out of six.
- Cuts and the beat grid. Phase test in the montage (24 cuts, 7.4 to 17.0 s). Tempo is 129 BPM from the tracker, but the strongest comb in the onset envelope is 196 BPM, and the phase concentration of the cuts is R = 0.04 on the 129 BPM grid and 0.23 on its half-beat grid, against a random-cut baseline of 0.18 with a 95th percentile of 0.34. That is no evidence of a grid. The earlier pass found the same by another route (31.2 ms mean offset against 29.1 ms by chance).
- Cuts and audio transients. 61 per cent of montage cuts sit within 50 ms of a detected transient, against 57 per cent for random cut times. The audio has about 6 transients per second, so this test cannot separate the two. I make no claim of sync to the sound effects from the audio alone. The creator's labels put the effects on the cuts and flashes.
- Captions and the voice. In the 10 fps frame sheet the words appear at 3.1 (WHAT), 3.2 (DO), 3.3 (YOU), 3.4 (PLAN) and 3.6 (TO DO) against transcript word starts of 3.06, 3.18, 3.26, 3.32 and 3.56 from the earlier pass. They land on the word, 0.04 to 0.08 s late, which is under two frames.

### Captions, what the higher resolution adds

Text is white, bold, uppercase and small, with a cap height of about 22 px at 720 px wide in a 408 px high band (about 5.4 per cent of the band). Lines hold one or two words. A function word joins the word it leans on, so the lines read WHAT / DO / YOU / PLAN / TO DO, IF / THIS WHOLE / DREAM / OF YOURS / DOESN'T / WORK OUT?, EVEN ENTER / MY. The accent word stands alone on its line, about 1.5 times bigger, in dark red (DREAM) or cyan with a split (CONSCIOUSNESS). The block moves between phrases to sit in the dark or empty part of the frame, right of centre over the cheek in the first phrase and left in the second. The earlier note says one word per line. Two words on a line is the common case here, and that matters for the engine's chunking.

### Sound design, what the earlier pass did not measure

Band levels every 50 ms (20 to 150 Hz, 150 Hz to 3 kHz, above 5 kHz) show a structure that the labels only hint at.

- 0.0 to 2.2 s: the low band is gated, about 0.2 s on and 0.1 s off, six off slots each 20 to 40 dB down. This is the creator's "tremolo". The off slots sit on or just before white frames.
- 2.4 to 5.1 s: under the voice the low band sits at 19 to 25 dB, against 44 to 52 dB everywhere else. About 24 dB of low end is removed for the speech. The mids are unchanged.
- 5.2 to 6.9 s: the low band climbs back (26 to 45 dB) with a high-frequency swell at 6.7 to 6.9. This is the creator's riser and charge. It is a build into the drop.
- 7.4 s: drop. The bed holds at 43 to 48 dB to 17.0 s.
- Stop-start hits. The whole mix drops 12 dB or more for 50 to 150 ms just before accents: 9.6 to 9.75 (hit at 9.8), 12.1 to 12.25 (hit at 12.3, the timeline's IMPACT clip starts there), 13.7 to 13.85, 14.5 to 14.7, and 17.1 to 17.65 before the end sting. The effect is that each hit lands out of near silence.
- 17.2 to 17.65 s: digital silence, then the sting.

The engine has a `gap` sfx kind that mutes the music, and it is used in the intro as a random 35 per cent of cuts. It is not placed ahead of the drop hit, because the sfx rule's `offset` is ignored for `gap` events in `render.mix_audio`. Nothing in the engine does a regular gate or removes bass under a voice.

## Ranked changes for the engine

Items 1 to 4 are implemented. Each sits behind a style option and is off by default. Nothing in an existing style changed. Proof is in `edits/tests/reels-2026-10-13/`: the plan (shots, events, caption words) is identical, all 394 video frames hash the same before and after (md5 756571e0...), and only the audio differs.

1. **Low-end duck under the voiceover.** `audio_shape.vo_low_duck` in `engine/audio_shape.py`, wired in `make_edit.py`. The music's low band (below 150 Hz) drops 20 dB across the voiceover and swells back over the last 1.8 s of the speech, mids untouched. Measured on the bw-flash-vo demo: low band under the voice -14.8 dB at the measuring point (a 2 s window that includes the ramp), mids 0.0 dB change, 0.0 dB change after the speech. This is the biggest audible difference. The voice sits in clean space and the return of the bass works as a build into the drop.
2. **Silence before hits.** `audio_shape.hit_gaps`. Adds a 0.1 s music mute that ends 0.02 s before each impact event of gain 0.4 or more, so the hit lands from silence. Works around the `offset` limit by writing the gap at the right time. In the demo it adds one gap at 7.38 s ahead of the drop impact.
3. **Intro gate.** `audio_shape.intro_gate`. A regular tremolo gate on the music during the intro, 3.3 Hz, 68 per cent on, -30 dB off, 5 ms edges, ending at the style's `intro_beats`. The demo shows the off slots reaching -50 to -64 dB per 50 ms against -33 for the music alone.
4. **Recorded effects with synthetic fallback.** `sfx_library` style option and `engine/sfx_library.py`. Events of a mapped kind get a `sample`, and `sfx.mix` plays the library clip if it loads and the synthesised effect if not. Events without `sample` are untouched.

Not yet done, in order of value.

5. `cuts.snap_words` for `bw-flash-vo`. The option exists in `make_edit.py` and the reel's cuts sit on word starts 6 of 6 times. Adding `"snap_words": {"tol": 0.25}` to the style would do it. I did not, because it would change existing renders and the style file already has uncommitted edits from another session. Size: one line.
6. A `vo_word` anchor for `place_rules` in `plan.py`, so whoosh, click and flash events can sit on a voiceover word start. Today `at` offers cut, shot, beat, drop, phrase, onset and time. Size: about 12 lines.
7. Caption line grouping in `captions.py` and `ext/kinetic.py`: join a function word to the next content word (TO DO, OF YOURS, THIS WHOLE, WORK OUT?), keep the accent word alone at 1.5 times size. The engine's `chunk_sizes` cycles fixed sizes and cannot do this. Size: a small stopword list and a chunker, about 25 lines.
8. `render.mix_audio` should honour `offset` on `gap` events and ramp the music back in over 2 ms to avoid a click. The file has another session's uncommitted changes, so this is for whoever owns that diff. Size: 3 lines.
9. A `hold` shape for `flash` in `fx.py`: seven white frames (0.23 s) at the drop, then decay. The engine's flash decays over 0.35 beat, which is shorter and softer. Size: 6 lines.
10. `check.py` should report the share of cuts within 0.07 s of a word start when a voiceover exists, and skip beat-grid scoring for styles marked as sound-led. Size: 15 lines.
11. Use the `ui` category clips (nextel, digital-text, counter) as `caption_sfx` options through `sfx_library`. The library's `counter` is the same kind of tick the reel puts under words. Size: style change only.
12. Emit a walkthrough layer (timeline strip with labelled clips and a moving playhead) from the edl. Medium, about a day.

## A3a pipeline (`vault/prep/ldms/a03a/render/make_video_v3.py`)

I did not edit this file. It is Cam's coursework pipeline and the changes below need a listen and a decision on tone. The reel is a flash-cut montage, and the A3a film is a fundraising piece about a forest fire, so strobes and gated bass are wrong for it. What carries over:

1. **Silence before the regrowth switch.** The strong switch at 29.9 s lands while the fire bed is still running. Mute the fire bed and the soft music for 0.1 to 0.25 s ahead of it, then fire the switch and open the music. This is the reel's stop-start hit and costs about 4 lines in the `gs` and `fire` arrays.
2. **A riser into 29.9 s.** The library `riser` is 3.76 s. Starting it at 26.1 s so it ends on the switch gives the build the section lacks. Use `lib.load('riser')` and place it like the clicks. The fire section has no music, so it will be heard clearly.
3. **Caption entry.** The captions fade in over 0.45 s. In the reel a word appears on the frame it is spoken and stays across cuts. Cut the fade-in to 0.1 s and keep the 0.4 s fade-out, so phrases start on their cut and still leave softly.
4. **One accent per caption.** Give "2 000 hectares", "48 heures" and "250 €" a warmer colour at 1.4 times size, with the rest in the base colour. The reel does this once per phrase and it is the only emphasis.
5. **Real click sounds.** The synthesised light switch could take `camera-click` or `counter` from the library at -6 dB, layered under the existing click. Check by ear first, because the clicks are the film's signature sound.
6. **Cuts follow the clip, not a grid.** The film already cuts on the AirDropped clip's own edit points. The reel shows no beat grid either, so there is nothing to change.

## Reproducing the measurements
