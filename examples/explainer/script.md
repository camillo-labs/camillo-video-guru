# Video engine explainer: script

1. I built a video engine that turns sources, a script and a voice into a finished short, and it made this film.
2. Feedparser and trafilatura read feeds and pages, and every number goes into a fact ledger.
3. I write a script from the ledger, and Kokoro narrates it on my own machine.
4. Faster-whisper times every word, and that timing drives the edit.
5. The engine is a set of styles: flash cuts, lyric words, talking head, kinetic cards. Each is a file of measured rules.
6. A planner finds the beats, and cuts land on the grid, or on word starts when a voice leads.
7. Captions are drawn with Pillow, frame by frame, because my ffmpeg has no text filter.
8. Sound is synthesised too, from whooshes to a light switch click, so nothing needs a licence.
9. One film took its cuts from a reference clip.
10. Then it renders to H.264 and checks its own cuts against the beat grid.
11. The review loop matters most. A fresh reviewer scores each render against the reference, and I change the engine, not the video. Major differences in one style fell from eight to none in three rounds.
12. Photo, vector, layout and motion apps feed it assets, and the voice is my own clone.
