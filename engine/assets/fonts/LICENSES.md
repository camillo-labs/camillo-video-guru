# Fonts

Anton, Bebas Neue, Archivo Black, Space Grotesk and Inter Tight come from the Google Fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts) and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Licence texts for Anton and Bebas Neue are in OFL-*.txt. The OFL permits embedding in rendered video and redistribution of the font files with the licence. Retrieved 12 October 2026.

Instrument Serif (Regular, Italic) and Playfair Display (Regular and Italic variable) were added on 12 October 2026 for the talking-head-split and kinetic-card-explainer styles. Both come from github.com/google/fonts under the SIL OFL 1.1, licence texts in OFL-InstrumentSerif.txt and OFL-PlayfairDisplay.txt.
