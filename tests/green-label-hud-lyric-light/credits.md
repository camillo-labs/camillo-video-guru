## Facts on screen and their sources

- "4 to 9% of textiles placed on the EU market are destroyed before use" (EEA, 2024): European Environment Agency briefing "The destruction of returned and unsold textiles in Europe's circular economy", 4 March 2024. The EEA authors say the overall size of textile product destruction "remains uncertain".
- "Since 19 July 2026 the EU bans large companies from destroying unsold clothes" (Regulation (EU) 2024/1781): the ban is phased, applies to large enterprises from 19 July 2026, medium-sized from 19 July 2030, and does not cover fabric or the UK domestic market.
