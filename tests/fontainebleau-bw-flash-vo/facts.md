# Château de Fontainebleau: caption facts and their sources

Each fact was checked on 12 October 2026 against the pages named. Quoted wording is exact.

## Facts cleared for captions

1. Used by the kings of France from the 12th century
   - UNESCO World Heritage Centre, list entry 160, "Palace and Park of Fontainebleau": "Used by the kings of France from the 12th century, the medieval royal hunting lodge of Fontainebleau, standing at the heart of a vast forest in the Ile-de-France, was transformed, enlarged and embellished in the 16th century by François I, who wanted to make a 'New Rome' of it."
   - Page: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/160 (the site blocks scripted requests, so the text was read from UNESCO's open-data record: https://data.unesco.org/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/whc001/records?where=id_no%3D160, field short_description_en).
   - Suggested caption: "Used by the kings of France from the 12th century. UNESCO"
2. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981
   - UNESCO open-data record for id_no 160: date_inscribed 1981, category Cultural, name "Palace and Park of Fontainebleau". Same URL as fact 1.
   - Corroboration, English Wikipedia "Palace of Fontainebleau" (revision 1375010186, 15 September 2026): "designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981".
   - Suggested caption: "UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981"
3. Earliest royal residence dated to 1137
   - English Wikipedia "Palace of Fontainebleau", section "Hunting Lodge and castle (12th century)": "The earliest reference to a royal residence at Fontainebleau dates to 1137 under King Louis VII the Younger, but it had probably been built earlier".
   - Caption only with the wording "earliest reference", not "built in 1137".
4. Location
   - Same article, lead: "located 55 kilometers (34 miles) southeast of the center of Paris, in the commune of Fontainebleau".
   - UNESCO: "standing in the heart of the vast forest of the Ile-de-France in the Seine-et-Marne region". Seine-et-Marne is also named in the same record's "Brief synthesis".
   - Suggested caption: "Fontainebleau, Seine-et-Marne, 55 km south-east of Paris"
5. Next to INSEAD
   - English Wikipedia "INSEAD": "a non-profit business school with locations in France (Fontainebleau), Singapore, the United Arab Emirates (Abu Dhabi), and the United States (San Francisco)".
   - Suggested caption: "Home town of INSEAD's Europe campus". Say town, not "next door", unless a distance is sourced from INSEAD's own site.
6. Napoleon's farewell to the Old Guard, 20 April 1814
   - English Wikipedia "Palace of Fontainebleau": "On 20 April, after surviving a suicide attempt, he gave an emotional farewell to the soldiers of the Old Guard, assembled in the Court of Honor." The preceding sentence gives the abdication as 4 April 1814.
   - French Wikipedia "Histoire de Fontainebleau" (revision 237849954, 15 July 2026, the dashes around "les célèbres grognards" are shown as brackets here): "Le 20 avril 1814, Napoléon, peu après sa première abdication, fait ses adieux à sa garde, (les célèbres grognards), dans la cour du Cheval blanc – devenue depuis « Cour des Adieux »".
   - UNESCO also names "the abdication of Napoleon I in 1814".
   - Suggested caption: "20 April 1814: Napoleon says farewell to the Old Guard in the Cour du Cheval-Blanc". The Horseshoe staircase stands in that courtyard, but none of the pages fetched says he stood on it, so do not caption that.
7. The Horseshoe staircase
   - English Wikipedia, Henry II section: "decorated it with a horseshoe-shaped staircase, which was later enlarged, and became a symbol of the château".
   - Louis XIII section: "assigned the court architect Jean Androuet du Cerceau to reconstruct the horseshoe stairway earlier designed by Philibert Delorme on the courtyard that had become known as the Cour de Cheval Blanc".
   - Suggested caption: "The Horseshoe staircase, a symbol of the château"
8. François I and the building from 1528
   - UNESCO record: "The construction of the palace began in 1528." and "François I, who wanted to make a 'New Rome' of it".
   - English Wikipedia: "Beginning in about 1528, Francis constructed the Galerie François I".
   - Suggested caption: "From 1528 François I builds his 'New Rome'"
9. Scale
   - Official site, https://www.chateaudefontainebleau.fr/en/ (page text): "900 years of history", "A 1,500-room château", "130 hectares of park and gardens".
   - Suggested caption: "1,500 rooms. 130 hectares of park and gardens. Château de Fontainebleau"

## Carp Pond and gardens

- French Wikipedia "Château de Fontainebleau", section "Étang des Carpes": "Au centre d'un vaste étang peuplé de carpes, dont les premiers spécimens, une soixantaine, furent offerts à Henri IV par Charles de Lorraine, s'élève le pavillon de l'Étang". Suitable for a caption such as "The Carp Pond, first stocked with about sixty carp given to Henri IV" if wanted.

## Not verified, do not caption

- That Napoleon stood on the Horseshoe staircase for the farewell (see fact 6).
- "Seven centuries of continuous royal occupation" and similar claims: not found in any page fetched. UNESCO says the palace was a royal or imperial residence "until the end of the Second Empire".
- The distance between the château and INSEAD: no source fetched.
