# lyric-word-beat, review round 1

Reference: `_source/ScreenRecording_10-11-2026 22-33-20_1.MP4`, band cropped to 1290x724 at y 911 and resampled to 30 fps (`review-round-1/ref_band.mp4`, 16.93 s).
Render: `rounds/round-1.mp4` (1280x720, 30 fps, 15.73 s, 122 BPM, 8 shots).
Method: side-by-side frame grids, per-frame mean luma (`review-round-1/*.yavg.txt`), ffmpeg scene detection (scene > 0.2), per-bar loudness, the round-1 EDL and `style.json`.

Root cause behind most caption findings: `style.json` has `captions.kind: "kinetic"` (font Anton, size_frac 0.15, chunk_sizes [1,1,2], depth_text enabled, fade in and out). The README and the engine's `lyric_diff` caption kind in `engine/ext/lyric_word_beat.py` describe the measured rules (one word, Arial Black, 0.096, hard swap, shadow, y_alt, linger), but the style never selects it. Switching the captions block back to `lyric_diff` closes D3 to D7, D22 and D30 in one move.

Scene cuts detected. Reference: 1.47, 2.00, 3.70 to 4.10 (haze cluster), 8.40 to 8.67 (radial cluster), 10.27, 12.50, 14.60, 16.43, 16.87. Render: 1.47, 1.50, 1.97, 4.43, 6.53, 9.83, 11.27, 13.77, 14.33 (silhouette strobe), 15.23, 15.53, 15.63.
